# Team access

GoHighLevel owns staff membership, invitations and CRM roles. Stripe owns plan and purchased quantity. Settings → Team & seats reads the current location's staff and lets a current CRM administrator assign calling access and select existing inbound queues. It does not create a charge, change a per-user plan, invite staff or invent routing teams.

The users.readonly scope is required. With the existing company credential, company search includes unrestricted agency administrators that HighLevel omits from its location filter. Results are filtered by actual location membership or explicitly unrestricted agency access; installations without a company credential use the bound location query. A bounded complete /users/search result is normalized to names, roles and calling permission; phone/email never enter this projection. Company/location/installation are resolved from authenticated context. Deleted, other-location, unknown-role and calling-disabled members cannot receive new access. A stale admin claim is insufficient: mutations refresh the CRM actor's role. See the official user-search contract: https://marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/ghl/users/search-users/index.html.

A revision plus immutable view digest protects the directory, seats, paid purchase generation and queue configuration. Mutations re-fetch the directory, lock the workspace, signed purchase and inbound authority in that order, and recheck the bound installation. Provider changes after that fetch are not an atomic transaction with PostgreSQL: explicit local revocation is immediate for new capacity actions, while external CRM membership is rechecked on the next administrator action or call authorization. There is no claim of instantaneous global CRM revocation.

The paid plan and capacity come from a confirmed claimed purchase bound to the same Stripe subscription, or from the subscription items recorded by verified Stripe webhooks for legacy non-public billing. A lagging purchase status cannot revoke seats when the authoritative subscription is active, trialing or within the configured past-due grace period. Pending or unsigned purchases, expired grace, cancellations and mismatched subscription IDs never create paid access. Legacy seats also require a verified subscription item matching the assigned plan. Competing admins cannot oversubscribe capacity. Purchased-seat changes remain in Stripe; this editor does not convert legacy subscriptions into public purchases.

Starter has one explicit durable grant, initially claimed by the first CRM-verified calling-enabled administrator. Other CRM staff cannot use this free authority. A second verified administrator can revoke that grant if its owner has lost administrator status, calling eligibility or CRM membership, and then claim the single freed grant. Removed owners remain visible as unavailable entries so recovery is possible; every revocation and replacement records an immutable audit event. An eligible owner cannot be displaced by another administrator through the recovery path. Assigned paid seats are checked before starting or accepting calls and on every ready-state heartbeat; going away, declining an offer and completing wrap-up remain available after revocation.

Saved queue membership updates the existing policy's profiles, retaining other reps and existing skills. New capacity is registered away using the same runtime defaults. Saving any access or queue change pauses that rep. A revoke disables access, removes routing membership and withdraws readiness, but retains any offer/connecting/connected/unknown/wrap-up owner and all durable commands. The normal carrier reconciliation and wrap-up protocol releases it. Reactivation never automatically starts receiving.

Migration 018 is additive and forward-only. Audit events contain actor/rep IDs, revision and selected queue IDs, with no phone numbers or CRM contact fields. Historical rollback fixtures remove only forward-only ledger markers inside owned disposable databases; production rollback guards remain intact.

The enabled PostgreSQL lane includes commercial/team.postgres.integration.test.ts. It covers named member projection, Starter authority/recovery and audit, legacy signed-item compatibility, active/trial/grace seat quantities, stale purchase status and generation, paid quantity races, forged scopes/plans, CRM role changes, installation removal, real routing membership and unknown-owner draining. Browser execution and live carrier proof remain separate QA levels.
