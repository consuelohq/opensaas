Overflow is the configured fallback when the primary queue cannot provide a timely answer. Depending on the number's policy, it can lead to another queue, a callback option or voicemail.

Business hours, holidays and emergency closure settings affect this decision. A number with no eligible staffed reps needs a deliberate fallback; repeatedly offering the same unanswerable call is not an overflow strategy.

## Voicemail

Voicemail is separate from ordinary call recording. If enabled, the caller must reach the configured voicemail path and hear the relevant disclosure. The message's retention and access policy should be confirmed by your administrator.

## Check the caller experience

Before advertising a number, test what the caller hears during open hours, after hours and when every rep is unavailable. Verify the actual fallback and the resulting stored state.

These are preview options, not guarantees for every workspace. For a request to be called back, use [callbacks](/inbound/callbacks/). For staffing, see [rep readiness](/inbound/readiness/).
