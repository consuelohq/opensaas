## Before you begin

Your administrator must enable inbound, associate the advertised number with a staffed queue, and configure your rep identity and answering devices. This remains a preview workflow.

## Go ready

1. Open Dialer through the installed GoHighLevel app.
2. Check the inbound operator panel and configured device health.
3. Select the healthy browser or forwarding-phone endpoints you intend to answer.
4. Click **Go ready**.
5. Wait for **Ready for inbound** in the authoritative state.

Clicking the button is not sufficient if the panel returns to Reconnecting. Follow [connection troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/connection/) and keep another staffed answer path available until the state is stable.

## Take a call

Accept the current offer on the intended device and wait for Connected. For phone screening, follow the spoken acceptance prompt. Once the conversation ends, complete wrap-up before taking another call.

Use **Go away** when you cannot take another offer. This does not terminate a conversation already in progress.
