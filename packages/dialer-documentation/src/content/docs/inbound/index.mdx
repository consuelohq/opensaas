:::note[Inbound preview]
Inbound requires administrator activation and live validation for your number. Do not assume it is enabled because the operator panel is visible.
:::

Inbound means the customer calls your advertised number. The customer is already on the phone; the router does not need to call them back simply to connect them to a rep.

The system resolves the number's queue, checks its policy, finds eligible rep capacity and offers the call. A rep can answer through a configured browser or forwarding phone. An accepted offer then needs a confirmed media bridge before it becomes a connected conversation.

If nobody can answer, the configured waiting, overflow, callback or voicemail policy decides what happens next. Those options must be explicitly enabled; they are not automatic promises made by the presence of a phone number.

Start with [how routing works](/inbound/routing/), then [prepare your devices and go ready](/inbound/readiness/).
