Outbound means your team starts the call. You can call one person directly or work through a pipeline-stage queue inside GoHighLevel.

## Choose your workflow

- Use **Single dial** for one known contact or phone number.
- Use a **pipeline-stage queue** to work through a group of eligible opportunities.
- Use additional lines when your plan and calling configuration permit them.

The browser sends your intent. The server selects eligible candidates, applies the calling policy and manages the provider lifecycle. With multi-line dialing, the selected connected person becomes the conversation; the remaining legs are cleaned up according to the server's state.

More lines do not guarantee a higher answer rate. The eligible pool, recipient behavior, carrier delivery and available rep capacity all matter.

Begin with [one test call](/start/first-call/), then [prepare a queue](/outbound/queues/). After each conversation, record the outcome in [wrap-up](/outbound/wrap-up/).
