When the conversation ends, record what happened while it is fresh. A disposition is a structured outcome such as a completed conversation or a needed follow-up; notes provide the context behind it.

## Finish a conversation

1. Confirm that the call has ended.
2. Choose the outcome that matches the conversation.
3. Add the next useful detail in notes, following your team's policy.
4. Submit wrap-up and wait for confirmation.
5. Continue the queue or return home.

A direct call returns to preparation after its wrap-up. A queue can continue to the next eligible batch or finish when no work remains. Do not record an answered conversation for a batch that never produced a connected person.

Inbound wrap-up also protects rep capacity: being online does not make you available while documentation is still in progress. If a save fails, preserve the notes and check the current state before repeating the action.

Use [history](/analytics/history/) to confirm the stored result.
