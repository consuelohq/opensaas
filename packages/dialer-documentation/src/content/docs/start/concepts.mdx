title: Core concepts
description: Understand contacts, queues, lines and call outcomes.

---

A **contact** is the person you want to reach. An **opportunity** links that contact to a deal and a pipeline stage in GoHighLevel.

A **queue** is the eligible work represented by a selected pipeline stage. The server checks eligibility and chooses the next candidates; opening a queue does not immediately call every contact in it.

A **line** is one concurrent outbound call attempt. Choosing three lines requests a batch of up to three eligible calls. It does not mean three reps will join one conversation, or that three calls will always be started.

An **inbound offer** asks an eligible rep to take a waiting caller. Accepting an offer starts connection work. The call is connected only after the media bridge is confirmed.

**Wrap-up** is the time after a conversation for recording the result and next step. A rep can be online while still occupied with another call or its wrap-up.

See [outbound calling](/outbound/) and [inbound routing](/inbound/routing/) for the two customer journeys.
