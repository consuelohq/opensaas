A rep needs access to the connected GoHighLevel location and a working answering device. An online account can still be unavailable because of an active call, reservation, wrap-up or device problem.

## Administrator preparation

Confirm which reps belong to each inbound queue, which devices they can use, the allowed caller numbers and the plan's capacity. Check business hours and fallback behavior before publishing a number.

## Rep preparation

1. Open Dialer in the correct location.
2. Check your microphone or forwarding-phone configuration.
3. For inbound preview, select healthy endpoints and [go ready](/inbound/readiness/).
4. Go away when you cannot accept another offer.
5. Complete wrap-up after the conversation.

Presence, readiness and capacity are different states. A browser reconnect does not prove that a carrier leg ended, and a call ending does not necessarily finish wrap-up.

See [browser and phone devices](/team/devices/) for answering choices and [billing](/billing/) for commercial seat configuration.
