Transcription converts authorized call audio to text. The current server integration uses **Groq** with the **Whisper** speech-to-text model; the configured default is `whisper-large-v3-turbo`.

It depends on workspace authorization, plan eligibility, media-stream configuration and provider availability. Connecting a call does not automatically enable transcription.

## Read transcript status

Where available, call details expose the transcript and its status. A disabled transcript differs from a failed one. A provider failure should not be interpreted as an empty conversation.

Speech recognition can mishear names, numbers or overlapping voices. Check context before relying on a critical detail, and follow your team's recording, access and retention policy.

## Planned coaching features

Live AI coaching, question-and-answer assistance and uploaded-transcript feedback for reps and leaders are **planned**. They are not promised as available self-service workflows in this release. Do not upload customer transcripts to an unrelated service to work around an unavailable feature.
