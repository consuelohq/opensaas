Begin with [call history](/analytics/history/): which calls started, which reached a connected conversation, which ended, and which need follow-up.

## Compare like with like

For outbound, distinguish attempts, provider answers and confirmed human conversations. A carrier answer can be voicemail. State the time range and denominator when discussing an answer or connection rate.

For inbound, consider whether callers reached a rep, how long they waited, whether they abandoned, and whether callbacks were completed. Platform answer is different from a rep conversation.

A decline, abandonment and provider failure describe different events. Do not treat all of them as an uninterested customer.

## Reporting scope

The application stores call lifecycle and transcript states. Available reports, exports and team dashboards depend on the deployed workspace; every metric discussed here does not necessarily have a finished dashboard.

[Transcripts and AI](/analytics/transcripts/) provide additional context when enabled. They do not replace connection and outcome evidence.
