Open call history in the connected Dialer and choose the relevant record. Check its time, participants, status and recorded outcome before starting follow-up work.

A multi-line session can contain several provider attempts. Read the session and its child attempts together: losing legs, unanswered calls and the selected conversation have different meanings.

## Verify a completed call

1. Locate the session for the intended contact and time.
2. Confirm the terminal call state.
3. Review the saved disposition and notes.
4. If transcription is enabled, check whether its status is ready, disabled or failed.

A missing transcript does not prove a call never happened. An accepted offer does not prove both media legs connected.

If a record appears stuck, capture its reference and approximate time, then ask your administrator to inspect provider and server state. Avoid restarting a call whose outcome is uncertain.

For read-only endpoints, see [HTTP API](/reference/http-api/).
