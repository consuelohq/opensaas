The current server catalog has three monthly tiers: **Single**, **Standard** and **Power**. Prices, permitted number quantities and usage settings are configuration-backed.

| Plan | Catalog behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Single | Configured included minutes and number limit. Predictive dialing, recording and transcripts are not enabled by this tier's catalog definition. |
| Standard | Predictive dialing, recording and transcript eligibility, subject to activation and the configured number limit. |
| Power | Predictive dialing, recording and transcript eligibility, with its separately configured price and number limit. |

Eligibility is different from activation. Recording and transcription still require configuration and authorization. The catalog does not establish a public promise of unlimited minutes for Standard or Power.

Check the billing screen for monthly prices, seats, included numbers, additional-number charges and trial limits. This preview does not publish prices from test fixtures or promise a new checkout flow.

See [feature availability](/start/availability/) before choosing a plan for a specific workflow.
