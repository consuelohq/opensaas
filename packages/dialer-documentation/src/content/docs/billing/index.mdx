An administrator manages the workspace's plan, paid seats, caller numbers and permitted features. The checkout or billing screen is the current quote for your account.

## Review a change

Check the monthly subscription, seat quantity, included usage, additional-number charges and any usage or provider costs. A monthly fee does not by itself mean every kind of usage is unlimited.

The calling account also needs working provider configuration. Funding a provider account and activating a subscription are separate prerequisites; neither establishes that a number is ready for traffic.

## Feature access

Plan rules can affect predictive dialing, recordings, transcripts and number capacity. See [compare plans](/billing/plans/) for the catalog structure. Verify live prices and entitlements rather than relying on an old screenshot.

Opening pricing does not purchase seats or provision a number. Confirm the displayed quantity and total before submitting a commercial action.
