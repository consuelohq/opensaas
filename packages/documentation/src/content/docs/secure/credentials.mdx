Consuelo credentials are generated for one workspace and caller purpose. They are not a shared password for the whole OS.

## Credential lifecycle

A credential moves through explicit states:

```text
issued → active → rotated or revoked
                    ↘ expired
```

- `active` credentials can be used until expiry or policy denies the request.
- `rotated` credentials have been replaced and are no longer accepted.
- `revoked` credentials were deliberately disabled and are no longer accepted.

Rotation creates new credential material and invalidates the old credential. Revocation is terminal for that credential ID.

## Raw material is write-only

At issue or rotation time, the caller may receive the private material needed to sign requests or an opaque bearer value. Current persisted status records keep verifier material or a hash plus scoped metadata—not the raw credential.

Status views may include the credential ID, workspace and caller bindings, scopes, state, expiry, and lifecycle timestamps. They must not become a secret-recovery endpoint.

Store newly issued material in the client or secret store that will use it. Do not paste it into steering, docs, task notes, traces, screenshots, or support messages.

## Generated local auth

A managed local installation creates Consuelo-specific security configuration in the OS home. Public or tunnel mode does not start without generated auth and workspace identity.

The old generic environment variable `MCP_BEARER_TOKEN` is **not accepted** as a fallback. A copied legacy token cannot silently bypass current workspace, caller, scope, expiry, rotation, or revocation checks.

## Per-request proof

Signed machine requests bind the credential to the method, path, request body, timestamp, and nonce. The gateway also checks the expected workspace and caller identities.

This prevents a captured signature from being reused for a different body or tenant. A nonce cannot be replayed inside the verification window, and an old timestamp is rejected.

## Bootstrap material is not durable identity

Device authorization can return short-lived connector bootstrap material after approval. That material configures the connector; it is not written into durable route registry rows or ordinary workspace configuration.

The installer also keeps connector bootstrap secrets out of generated auth metadata and Caddy output. Platform routing records identify the route and policy without storing the tunnel credential.

## Rotate or revoke

Rotate when:

- a client changes owners or machines;
- a scope set changes materially;
- a credential may have been exposed;
- the app needs fresh signing material.

Revoke when:

- the app or device is no longer trusted;
- the connection is removed;
- a workspace owner needs immediate termination.

After rotation or revocation, verify the old credential receives `TOKEN_ROTATED` or `TOKEN_REVOKED` and the replacement has only the intended scopes.

## Do not log secrets

Audit events record safe identity, scope, action, status, and timing fields. They should not include raw credentials, private keys, bearer tokens, request bodies, tunnel origins, or local absolute paths.
